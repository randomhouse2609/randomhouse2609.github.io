Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Command Centre is a private assistant used only by its owner, Aniruddh Mehta, for his own Google accounts.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, it reads, searches, drafts and sends email (Gmail) and reads and creates calendar events (Google Calendar) in the accounts the owner has signed in with.

How the data is used

The data is used only to carry out the owner's own instructions, such as summarising mail, drafting replies he approves, and adding events. Emails and invitations are sent only after the owner approves each one.

Where the data goes

The sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Message content may be processed by the AI service the owner uses to run the assistant, only to answer his requests.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from Google Account → Security → Your connections to third-party apps & services.

Google API data

Command Centre's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
